---
id: citation-integrity
violation_type: markdown:citation-integrity
summary: An in-text citation does not resolve to a dated entry in the document's references.
---

## Why

An author-date citation is a pointer. It is only useful if the reader can follow it to a reference
entry that names the same authors and the same year. A citation with no matching entry, or an entry
missing its year, cannot be followed. The reader cannot check the claim it supports, and cannot tell
whether the reference exists at all or was misremembered.

## Corrective move

Make each citation and its reference entry match mechanically. Add the missing entry to the
references section, with authors, year and title, or correct the in-text citation so its authors and
year match an existing entry. If the source cannot be identified, remove the citation and state the
claim without it, or mark the claim as unsourced.

## Expected outcome

Every in-text citation resolves to exactly one reference entry, and every entry carries a
publication year. A reader can go from any citation to its source without guessing.

## Avoid

Adding a placeholder entry that matches the citation's text but describes no existing source. That
clears the check and leaves the claim with nothing behind it.
